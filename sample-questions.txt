Q: What is the supreme law of the land in many constitutional democracies?
A: Statutory law
B: The Constitution
C: Case law
D: Customary law
Answer: B
Explanation: The Constitution is typically the supreme law that all other laws must conform to.
---
Q: Which legal principle means "let the decision stand"?
A: Habeas Corpus
B: Stare Decisis
C: Prima Facie
D: Res Judicata
Answer: B
Explanation: Stare decisis is the doctrine that courts should follow precedent.
---
Q: What is the standard of proof in criminal cases?
A: Preponderance of evidence
B: Clear and convincing evidence
C: Beyond reasonable doubt
D: Probable cause
Answer: C
Explanation: Criminal cases require proof beyond reasonable doubt, the highest standard.
---
Q: Which amendment protects against self-incrimination in the US Constitution?
A: 4th Amendment
B: 5th Amendment
C: 6th Amendment
D: 8th Amendment
Answer: B
Explanation: The 5th Amendment includes protection against self-incrimination.
---
Q: What does "mens rea" refer to in criminal law?
A: The physical act of the crime
B: The guilty mind or intent
C: The crime scene
D: The victim's statement
Answer: B
Explanation: Mens rea refers to the mental state or intent behind a criminal act.
